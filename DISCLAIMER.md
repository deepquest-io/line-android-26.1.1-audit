# Disclaimer & Responsible-Use Notice

This repository contains security-research material: a static-analysis audit of a
publicly distributed Android application, supporting documentation, and
proof-of-concept **analysis** scripts.

## Purpose

Published for **educational and defensive purposes** — to help developers and security
engineers understand real-world mobile hardening gaps and how to detect them. The PoC
scripts are detection and analysis tooling; they are **not** working exploits and do
not attack live services.

## Authorized use only

Use these tools and techniques **only** against:

- applications you own, or
- applications you have **explicit written authorization** to test.

Unauthorized testing of third-party applications or infrastructure may violate the
Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), the UK Computer Misuse Act, GDPR, and equivalent
laws in your jurisdiction.
